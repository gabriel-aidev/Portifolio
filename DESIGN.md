# Design System: Gabriel Maciel - Portfólio

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Night Depot"**

A near-black, green-tinged ground (`bg`) with three fixed radial glows in the corners, over which content sits on translucent surfaces that are barely lighter than the page and are defined by a 1px white-at-9% hairline rather than by fill. One accent, a bright signal green, is spent on small things: the underline of the active nav link, the icon inside a badge, the border of a hovered tile, the text of the primary pill button, the fill of the progress rail. Everything else is greys with a green undertone. The mood is a lit warehouse at night: quiet, structured, one colour of light.

Density is editorial rather than dashboard-like. Text columns are capped at 60–70ch, section titles are followed by a fading hairline, headings are tight-tracked Poppins, numbers are tabular. Depth is conveyed by borders and a soft ambient shadow, with a green glow reserved for hover and focus. Illustrations are single-stroke line drawings in `text-muted` with one green element, drawn over a faint 28px grid panel.

Motion is a single easing curve (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`) at three durations (160/280/460ms), fade-up entrances of 600–700ms, a hover sheen sweep on pill buttons, and a stroke draw-in on the illustrations. Every animation is declared inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`, so with reduced motion nothing is ever at opacity 0: content is born visible.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One accent (`#3DDC5A`) used at small scale; large areas are never green.
- Surfaces are translucent white at 3.5% with a 9% hairline; hover lifts to 6% and strengthens the border.
- Two container recipes: informational (`surface` + `border` + `text-muted`) and actionable (`accent-deep` + `border-strong` + `accent`).
- Pill radius on every interactive element; 18/12/8px on containers by size.
- Poppins only, 300–700 loaded, 400–700 used; negative tracking on headings, tabular numerals on dates.
- Motion lives only inside the no-preference gate; one easing curve everywhere.

## Colors

A monochrome green-black ground with one signal green; the rest is alpha-white layering and three text greys.

### Primary
- **Signal Green** (`accent`): the single accent. Active nav underline and text, hover borders, icon tint inside badges and tech tiles, primary button text, rail fill, active step dot, `::selection` at 28% alpha, focus outline (2px, offset 3px).
- **Moss Well** (`accent-deep`): the fill of actionable pills (primary buttons, status chip, active module button). Always paired with `border-strong` and `accent` text.
- **Green Haze** (`accent-glow`): 25% accent. Radial glow in the corner of grid panels and project covers, the hero halo, and the 5px ring around the active step dot.
- **Strong Hairline** (`border-strong`): 35% accent. The border of actionable pills at rest, and the hover border of informational cards and badges.

### Neutral
- **Depot Black** (`bg`): page background, `<meta theme-color>`, `<html>` pre-paint colour, step-dot fill, scrollbar track.
- **Depot Black Soft** (`bg-soft`): fill of illustration/grid panels and video frames.
- **Breath Surface** (`surface`): 3.5% white. Cards, chips, badges, tech tiles, icon pills.
- **Breath Surface Hover** (`surface-hover`): 6% white. Hover state of tiles and rows; the resting fill of secondary pill buttons.
- **Solid Slate** (`surface-solid`): opaque fallback surface for the mobile menu, the MUI modal and the certificate frame.
- **Hairline** (`border`): 9% white. Every container border, dividers inside cards, the rail track, the resting step-dot ring.
- **Paper** (`text`): headings, body of the active step, strong text.
- **Ash** (`text-muted`): default paragraph colour, nav links at rest, chip and badge text, illustration ink.
- **Dust** (`text-faint`): uppercase group labels, role lines, stack labels.

### Named Rules
**The Two Recipes Rule.** A container is either informational (`surface` fill, `border` hairline, `text-muted` text) or actionable (`accent-deep` fill, `border-strong` hairline, `accent` text). Do not mix the halves; a badge is never green-filled and a button is never grey-filled with green text.

**The Border Speaks Rule.** State is communicated through `border-color` before anything else: rest `border` → hover `border-strong` (informational) or `accent` (actionable, tiles, contact rows). Fills move at most one step (`surface` → `surface-hover`).

**The Small Green Rule.** The accent is a highlight, not a field: the largest green area on any screen is a pill button or a 42px icon circle. Large green fills, green headings and green backgrounds do not exist in this system; the heading gradient (`#F2F7F3` → `#A1CCA5` → `#3DDC5A`) is the only place green touches display type, and only on the home hero.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Poppins (with sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Poppins (with sans-serif)

Loaded from Google Fonts at 300, 400, 500, 600, 700 (`display=swap`); 300 is loaded but not used in any component. Applied globally through the universal selector, so every element inherits it.

**Character:** Geometric and even; the system leans on weight and tracking rather than a second face. Headings are bold and tightened, body is regular with generous leading, labels are small and spaced out.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, `clamp(32px, 5vw, 56px)`, 1.1–1.15, -0.02em): page h1 (home hero, "Minha trajetória"). Home drops to `clamp(28px, 6vw, 40px)` under 800px. The home h1 optionally wears the heading gradient via `background-clip: text`.
- **Display-numeric** (700, `clamp(28px, 3.2vw, 44px)`, 1, -0.03em, tabular-nums): the period in the sticky story panel and the project cover name (`clamp(30px, 4vw, 46px)`). Dates always set `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.
- **Headline** (700, `clamp(24px, 3vw, 32px)`, 1.15, -0.02em, `text-wrap: balance`): case-study h3.
- **Title** (600, `clamp(22px, 2.6vw, 30px)`, 1.2, -0.02em): section titles (with trailing hairline) and story step h2.
- **Subtitle** (600, 22px): project card h2 (19px under 480px); 700/23px for the header logotype.
- **Body-lead** (400, 17px, 1.7, `text-muted`): intro paragraphs under the h1, max 65–70ch.
- **Body** (400, 16px, 1.7–1.75): case summaries, step paragraphs, max 60–70ch; 15px under 480–600px.
- **Body-small** (400, 14–15px, 1.5–1.6, `text-muted`): card descriptions, taglines, practice notes.
- **Nav** (400, 20px): desktop nav links; mobile menu items.
- **Control** (500, 14–15px): pill button and module button text.
- **Chip** (500, 12–12.5px, 0.02em on badges): tags, badges, tech tile names.
- **Label** (600, 12px, 0.08em, uppercase, `text-faint`): group labels inside cards and the tech grid ("Aprendizados", "Stack", tech group names). This is a group heading for the content that follows it, never a kicker above a title.
- **Caption** (400, 12–13px, `text-muted`/`text-faint`): role lines, contact labels, facts rows, copy feedback.

### Named Rules
**The Tight Heading Rule.** Any Poppins set at 22px or larger carries negative tracking: -0.02em by default, -0.03em at 44px and up. Body text and labels never do.

**The Tabular Date Rule.** Periods, years and figures use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` so the sticky panel and fact rows do not jitter when the number changes.

## Layout

Single centred column: `Container` is 90vw wide, 85vw from 800px, capped at 1320px from 1500px. The header is fixed, 85px tall, translucent with 14px backdrop blur; page content starts with the 85px offset plus 40–65px of breathing room (`padding-top: 125px` on /sobre, 150px on /contato, `margin-top: 85px; padding-top: 50px` on home).

Breakpoints actually used: 350 (logotype shrink), 450/480 (mobile type and padding), 600 (case-study compact), 620 (nav switches from burger to inline), 690/700 (single column, stacked hero), 800 (h1 clamp change, container width), 900 (two-column cards and the story grid collapse to one column), 968 (module button compact), 1500 (container cap). Desktop nav appears at 620px and up; below that, react-burger-menu opens a full-height `surface-solid` drawer over an 88% `bg` overlay.

Composition grids observed: hero is two halves (text max 620px + image 50%); case study body is `minmax(0,1fr) 300px` with a 40px gutter; project card is `1.05fr 1fr` with 28px gutter, max 1000px; story is `0.9fr 48px 1fr` with a 24px gutter (sticky panel at `top: 110px`, 2px rail centred in the 48px column, 16px dots), collapsing to `28px 1fr` under 900px with the illustration moving inline above each step.

Spacing rhythm: 6/8/10/12 for inline gaps and chip padding, 14–18 for card inner gaps, 20–24 for card padding on small screens and list gaps, 28–32 for card padding and section-title gaps on desktop, 40 for grid gutters and section margins, 60–80 for section bottoms, 64 between story steps. Content measure is capped at 60–70ch.

## Elevation & Depth

Hybrid, border-first. Surfaces are distinguished from the ground by a 1px `border` hairline and a 3.5% white fill, not by shadow. Two black ambient shadows give resting containers a little lift, and one green glow is reserved for state. The header and the contact card add `backdrop-filter: blur(12–14px)` so the fixed page glows show through.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Soft** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`): resting case-study cards and the course-projects panel.
- **Card** (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`): resting project cards, the story panel, contact card, portrait clips.
- **Glow** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(61, 220, 90, 0.35), 0 12px 36px rgba(61, 220, 90, 0.18)`): hover of project cards, contact icon links and contact rows (`:hover, :focus-within`). Never at rest.

### Named Rules
**The Glow-On-State Rule.** The green glow shadow appears only in response to hover or focus-within. At rest a surface has `soft`, `card`, or no shadow; the hairline border does the separating.

**The Lift Rule.** Hover lifts by transform, never by shadow growth alone: `translateY(-2px)` on pill buttons, `-3px` on icon pills, `-4px` on cards and tiles, `translateX(4px)` on contact rows.

## Shapes

Every interactive element is a pill (`999px`): buttons, chips, status badges, icon circles, module buttons, scrollbar thumb, rail and dots. Containers use three steps by size: `18px` for cards, panels, tiles and the modal; `12px` for media frames, contact rows, inline figures, copy buttons and cards when compacted under 600–690px; `8px` for the date badge and the global `:focus-visible` outline. Borders are always 1px (2px only on the story rail, step dot ring and the illustration stroke). The only decorative surface texture is the 28px grid (two 1px `rgba(255,255,255,0.035)` line gradients offset -1px) with an `accent-glow` radial in the top-right corner, used on illustration panels and private-project covers. Illustrations are 2px round-capped strokes in `currentColor` (`text-muted`) with one `accent` element, on a 400×300 viewBox, `pathLength="1"` for draw-in.

## Components

### Buttons
Pill links, quiet at rest, that brighten their border and rise on hover; a white sheen sweeps across in 750ms.
- **Shape:** pill (`999px`), 1px border, inline-flex with 8px icon gap, 16px icons.
- **Primary:** `accent-deep` fill, `border-strong` border, `accent` text; padding `11px 22px` (story actions), `9px 18px` (card links) or `12px 24px` (GitHub callout); 14–15px / 500. Carries `sheenOnHover`.
- **Hover / Focus:** border becomes `accent`, `translateY(-2px)`, transitions at 160ms. Focus uses the global 2px `accent` outline offset 3px.
- **Secondary:** `surface-hover` fill, `border` hairline, `text` text; hover border `border-strong`, same lift. No sheen.
- **Module button (Projects page):** full-width pill, `14px 20px`, `surface`/`border`/`text` at rest; active flips to the primary recipe and stays lifted `-2px`; chevron rotates 180°.
- **Icon pill:** 42px circle (36px under 480px), `surface` fill, `border`; hover border `accent`, fill `surface-hover`, `glow` shadow, `-3px` lift, icon turns `accent`. Contact page uses the same circle with the icon already `accent`.

### Chips
- **Style (tag):** `surface` fill, `border` hairline, `text-muted` text, `4px 10px`, 12–12.5px / 500, pill.
- **Status chip:** primary recipe (`accent-deep` / `border-strong` / `accent`), `5px 12px`, 12px / 600 / 0.02em.
- **Date badge:** informational recipe, but `8px` radius instead of pill, `5px 12px` (compact `3px 9px`, 11px), 13px Feather calendar icon in `accent`; hover strengthens the border and lifts text to `text`. Non-clickable, `cursor: default`.
- **MUI Chip (Projects page):** `variant='outlined' size='small'` styled through `chipSx` from theme tokens; a leftover from the old build, kept visually aligned with the tag chip.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** `18px`; `12px` under 600–690px.
- **Background:** `surface`; illustration panels and media frames use `bg-soft` with the 28px grid and corner glow.
- **Shadow Strategy:** `soft` for text cards, `card` for media cards and panels, `glow` on hover of media cards.
- **Border:** 1px `border`; hover `border-strong`.
- **Internal Padding:** 32px case study (22px 18px compact), 20px project card (16px under 480px), 24px course panel, 24px 28px story panel, 18px contact card.
- **Internal dividers:** 1px `border` top-borders between learning rows and before the facts row; uppercase `label` group headings with 14px margin below.

### Tech grid
Tiles `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(108px, 1fr))` with 12px gap (92px / 10px under 480px); each tile is `surface`/`border`/`18px`, 104px min height, 28px Simple Icons glyph in `text` above a 12px / 500 name in `text-muted`. Hover: `-4px` lift, `accent` border, `surface-hover` fill, icon to `accent`, name to `text`. Groups reveal in 600ms fade-ups staggered by 110ms.

### Navigation
- **Header:** fixed, 85px, `rgba(9, 12, 10, 0.72)` with 14px blur, bottom hairline `border`. Logotype 23px / 700 `text` (18px under 350px), turns `accent` on hover.
- **Links:** 20px / 400 `text-muted`, 25px apart; a 2px `accent` underline grows from 0 to 100% width in 280ms on hover; active link is `accent` with full underline.
- **Mobile (under 620px):** burger bars in `text` (hover `accent`), drawer in `surface-solid` with 20px items in `text`, hover `accent`, overlay `rgba(9, 12, 10, 0.88)`.

### Story scrollytelling (signature)
Three-column grid: a sticky grid panel (`bg-soft`, 28px grid, corner glow, `card` shadow, 18px radius) holding the tabular period in `display-numeric`, a 4:3 illustration area whose figures cross-fade over 460ms, and a 14px / 500 `text-muted` caption; a 2px rail (`border` track, `accent` fill scaling from the top, 120ms linear) with 16px dots (`bg` fill, 2px `border` ring → `accent` ring when reached → `accent` fill plus 5px `accent-glow` ring when active); and steps of `min-height: clamp(360px, 52vh, 520px)` whose h2 and paragraphs move from `text-muted` to `text` when active. Under 900px the panel disappears and each step gets an inline 12px-radius grid figure with a 22px / 700 tabular period. Illustrations draw in over 900ms with 55ms per-path stagger, only inside the no-preference gate.

### Inputs / Fields
No text inputs exist. The only field-like control is the contact copy button: 38px square, `12px` radius, `border` hairline, `text-muted` icon; hover and copied state turn icon and border `accent`, fill `surface-hover`; a 13px `accent` "copiado" feedback fades in beside it.

### Modal (leftover)
MUI `Modal` + `Box` styled from theme tokens: 400px (max 90vw), `surface-solid`, `border`, `18px` radius, 24px padding, `text`. Links inside are `accent` and underlined.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** build every container from the informational recipe (`surface` + `border` + `text-muted`) and every action from the actionable recipe (`accent-deep` + `border-strong` + `accent`).
- **Do** express hover through `border-color` first, then one fill step (`surface` → `surface-hover`), then a 2–4px `translateY` lift; use the `glow` shadow only on hover or focus-within.
- **Do** use pill radius on anything clickable and 18/12/8px on containers by size.
- **Do** set headings at 22px+ with -0.02em tracking (-0.03em from 44px) and dates with `tabular-nums`.
- **Do** declare every `animation` and every `opacity: 0` start state inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`; use `enter()`, `revealable`, `revealStagger` and `sheenOnHover` from `src/styles/motion.ts` and the theme's `transitions.fast/base/slow`.
- **Do** author new illustrations as inline SVG on the 400×300 viewBox, class `ink` (2px, round caps, `currentColor`), one `accent` element, `pathLength={1}`.
- **Do** use react-icons (Feather `fi` for UI, Simple Icons `si` for the tech grid) at 15–19px, tinted `accent` only when they are the signal inside a neutral element.
- **Do** keep text columns at 60–70ch and respect the fixed 85px header with `scroll-margin-top: 100px` on anchors.

### Don't:
- **Don't** fill large areas with the accent, colour headings green, or add a second accent hue; the hero heading gradient is the only green display treatment.
- **Don't** use the legacy palette layer in `theme.ts` (`grey*`, `secondary*`, `backGreen*`, `primary*`); it is retained as a safety net and no component consumes it.
- **Don't** use opaque light surfaces or borders heavier than 1px on containers; separation comes from the 9% hairline, not from fill contrast.
- **Don't** ship a resting drop shadow in green, a hard offset shadow, or a shadow as the only hover response.
- **Don't** write animations outside the reduced-motion gate or rely on the global duration-zeroing rule to un-hide `fill-mode: both` entrances.
- **Don't** introduce a second typeface, a system display face, uppercase kickers above headings, or letter-spaced labels larger than 12px; the uppercase `label` is a group heading inside a card, not an eyebrow.
- **Don't** add new MUI components; the Chip and Modal on the Projects page are a leftover styled from tokens, not a pattern to extend.
